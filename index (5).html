About Cricket Training Timer

Cricket Training Timer is a tool for managing live batting time in the nets at cricket training. Add players as they arrive, open your nets, and the app allocates batting time for each player and counts down every turn.

  • Batting time allocated live, net by net
  • Allocations update as players arrive or leave and nets open or close
  • Minimum and maximum batting times
  • Pad-up alerts and changeover time between batters
  • Start batting at a set time, or when enough players arrive
  • Synchronise nets so they change over together
  • Session reports with CSV export
