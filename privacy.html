Privacy Policy

Cricket Training Timer · Effective 12 October 2026

Cricket Training Timer does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information you enter

The app lets you enter a club name, your name (optional), player names, optional player email addresses and roles, training session settings, and batting times. All of this is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it.

No accounts, analytics or advertising

The app has no sign-in or user accounts. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules pad-up and session alerts locally on your device. No information is sent to a server to deliver them.

Backups and exports

Your app data may be included in the iCloud or computer backups you make of your device, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy. Session reports leave your device only when you choose to export or share a CSV file yourself.

Children

Coaches or session managers may enter the names of junior players. That information stays on the coach's device and is not collected by us.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete all data at any time in Settings → Delete All Data, or by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
